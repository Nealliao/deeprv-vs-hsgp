# Data sources

All raw sources are public. Processed panels small enough to track are included in the repository;
the raw downloads are not.

## ECDC measles

Confirmed measles cases by country and month, 2010–2019.

- **Source:** European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control, measles monthly surveillance.
  <https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/measles/surveillance-and-disease-data>
- **Exposure:** Eurostat population on 1 January, total population
  (online data code `demo_pjan`), retrieved through the Eurostat REST API.
  <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/demo_pjan>
- **Processed panel (included):** `experiments/04_ecdc_measles/data/ecdc_measles_panel.csv`
  — 29 countries × 120 months, after dropping the pan-European aggregate row and one country with
  no cases in the window.
- **Build script:** `experiments/04_ecdc_measles/scripts/110_prepare_ecdc_measles_panel.py`

## Project Tycho measles

US notifiable-disease records, pre-vaccine era 1928–1962, aggregated to months.

- **Source:** van Panhuis et al. (2013), Project Tycho. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMms1215400>
- **Panel:** 49 states × 420 months (`n = 19,871` after excluding reporting gaps).
- **Build script:** `experiments/05_tycho_measles/scripts/35_prepare_tycho_measles_monthly.py`
- Not included in the repository: the raw Tycho export is large and carries its own terms of use.
  Download it from Project Tycho and run the build script.

## Synthetic panel

Fully generated by script — no download needed. 20 regions × 72 months, Poisson counts, latent field
drawn at full rank by exact Cholesky factorisation with a doubly centred interaction.

- **Generator:** `experiments/01_synthetic_benchmark/scripts/`
- True hyperparameters: `(α_g, ℓ_g) = (0.30, 0.55)`, `(α_q, ℓ_q) = (0.20, 18)` months,
  `(α_w, ℓ_ws, ℓ_wt) = (0.18, 0.15, 6)`.
